Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. When you upgrade, features become available immediately. When you downgrade, your plan change takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

What happens if I go over my plan?

We don't throttle or surprise you with overages. If your usage is growing, we'll reach out to help you find the right plan.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes. Reach out to sales@fabsdatawarehouse.com with details about your project and we'll work with you.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept card payments via Stripe. Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice with net-30 terms.

Can I host on my own infrastructure?

Yes — FABS Deploy is built to run on your own VPS. Enterprise customers get deployment support and dedicated infrastructure.
