FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free tier is permanent. Upgrade to Starter, Pro, or Business only when you need more resources or team seats.

Which languages and frameworks are supported?

Anything that can run in Docker. Auto-detection covers Flask, Django, FastAPI, Node.js, Next.js, Express, and more. For anything else, add a Dockerfile to your repo root and we'll use it directly.

How does auto-deploy work?

We install a GitHub webhook on your repo. Every push to your configured branch triggers a fresh build and zero-downtime container swap. You can also click Deploy manually from the dashboard at any time.

Can I bring my own domain?

Yes. Any subdomain works out of the box. Point your DNS at our server, add the domain in the dashboard, and we'll issue a valid Let's Encrypt SSL certificate within seconds.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your projects drop to the Free tier limits. Nothing is deleted immediately — you get a grace period to re-subscribe. Data is preserved for 30 days after cancellation.

Can I export my data?

Always. Database dumps, environment variables, and container images can be exported at any time. There's no lock-in — your code lives in your GitHub, not ours.
