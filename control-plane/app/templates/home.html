Talk to us

Questions, answered.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free tier is permanent. Upgrade to Starter, Pro, or Business only when you need more resources or team seats.

Which languages are supported?

Anything that can run in Docker. Auto-detection covers Flask, Django, FastAPI, Node.js, Next.js, Express, and more. For anything else, add a Dockerfile.

How does auto-deploy work?

We install a GitHub webhook on your repo. Every push to your configured branch triggers a fresh build and zero-downtime container swap.
